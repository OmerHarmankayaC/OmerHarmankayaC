# Ömer Harmankaya

Industrial & Computer Engineering student at TED University, Ankara.
I work where operations research meets software — scheduling, forecasting,
retrieval — and I care about shipping things end to end rather than leaving
them as notebooks.

**Currently**

- Building a constraint-programming shift scheduler for security personnel at
  BOTAŞ facilities: OR-Tools CP-SAT, FastAPI, React, PostgreSQL.
- Product lead for [Vera Finance](https://apps.apple.com/tr/app/vera-finance/id6760785410),
  a published iOS personal finance app — plus its
  [RAG Q&A assistant](https://rag.omerharmankaya.com).
- Training a Turkish sentence embedding model from scratch: synthetic pair
  generation, contrastive training on BERTurk, honest evaluation against a
  human-written gold set.
